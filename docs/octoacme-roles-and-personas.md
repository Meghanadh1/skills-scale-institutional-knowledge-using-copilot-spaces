# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Technical Lead**: Receive architectural guidance and design review feedback; escalate technical impediments
- **With QA/Testing Lead**: Collaborate on test plans; address quality issues and acceptance criteria gaps
- **With Project Manager**: Participate in planning, estimation, and status updates; report blockers and risks

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Sponsor/Stakeholder**: Align on business objectives and priorities; secure stakeholder approvals
- **With Project Manager**: Collaborate on release planning and milestone tracking
- **With QA/Testing Lead**: Define acceptance criteria and quality expectations; validate feature readiness

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Sponsor/Stakeholder**: Provide escalations, approvals, and resource support; communicate executive-level status
- **With Technical Lead**: Coordinate technical dependencies and technical risk mitigation
- **With QA/Testing Lead**: Track quality metrics and release readiness; coordinate testing schedules
- **With Scrum Master**: Align on team velocity, sprint planning, and process improvements

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, acceptance criteria validation, and test execution. They collaborate with developers and product managers to ensure features meet quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans and QA approach for each project phase
- Validate acceptance criteria and identify gaps before development starts
- Execute unit, integration, and end-to-end test plans
- Triage quality issues and manage defect workflow
- Coordinate manual QA and acceptance testing
- Participate in release readiness reviews
- Track test coverage and quality metrics

### Goals
- Prevent defects from reaching production
- Ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Enable confident, low-risk releases

### Typical Communication
- Quality metrics and test reports in weekly syncs
- Defect summaries and release readiness assessments
- Acceptance criteria validation feedback to developers

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Collaborate on test plans and acceptance criteria; review code for testability
- **With Product Manager**: Define and clarify acceptance criteria; validate feature completion
- **With Project Manager**: Provide quality status for release gates and milestone tracking
- **With Technical Lead**: Coordinate integration testing and technical quality standards

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide architectural decisions, evaluate technical feasibility, and manage technical risks. They enable the team to build scalable, maintainable solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct design reviews and propose architectural approaches
- Assess technical feasibility and effort estimates
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Mentor developers on code quality and best practices
- Oversee integration with other systems and services
- Approve technical documentation and design decisions
- Track technical debt and quality standards

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Enable knowledge transfer and team growth

### Typical Communication
- Design review feedback and architectural documentation
- Technical risk assessments and mitigation plans
- Code quality and best practices guidance

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Provide architectural guidance, mentor on design patterns and best practices
- **With Project Manager**: Escalate technical risks and dependencies affecting timelines
- **With QA/Testing Lead**: Define technical quality standards and testing approaches
- **With Sponsor/Stakeholder**: Explain technical trade-offs and feasibility constraints

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove team impediments, and drive process improvement. They enable the team to work efficiently and continuously improve their delivery practices.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove team impediments and blockers
- Track team velocity and sprint metrics
- Coach team members on agile practices and principles
- Identify and drive process improvements
- Protect team focus and manage distractions

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and flow
- Foster continuous improvement culture
- Enable self-organizing teams

### Typical Communication
- Sprint metrics and velocity trends
- Retrospective action items and improvements
- Facilitation of agile ceremonies

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Project Manager**: Coordinate sprint planning with project timelines; escalate external blockers
- **With Developers**: Facilitate ceremonies; remove impediments to progress
- **With All Roles**: Coach on agile practices; facilitate cross-functional collaboration

---

## Sponsor / Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide business context, approve key decisions, and ensure alignment with organizational priorities. They remove barriers and advocate for the project.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success metrics
- Approve project charter and release timelines
- Participate in key decision gates (initiation, planning, release)
- Escalate blockers and provide resource support
- Communicate project status to executive leadership
- Support team and remove organizational barriers
- Validate business value delivery

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value
- Maintain organizational alignment and priority
- Enable efficient resource allocation

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and briefings
- Decision gate approvals and feedback
- Executive-level status reports

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Project Manager**: Provide escalation, approvals, and resource support; receive status updates
- **With Product Manager**: Align on strategic priorities and success metrics
- **With Technical Lead**: Understand technical trade-offs and feasibility constraints
- **With All Roles**: Remove organizational barriers and provide executive sponsorship

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure projects meet security and regulatory requirements. They provide guidance on security best practices, conduct reviews, and manage incident response.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements and compliance standards
- Conduct security reviews during planning and architecture phases
- Coordinate security scanning and vulnerability assessments
- Manage security incidents and response coordination
- Track compliance with regulatory and organizational policies
- Provide security training and best practices guidance
- Maintain security documentation and audit trails

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and compliance violations
- Enable secure, compliant product delivery
- Maintain customer trust and organizational reputation

### Typical Communication
- Security requirements and design review feedback
- Vulnerability assessments and remediation plans
- Incident response coordination and post-mortems
- Compliance audit reports and policy updates

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Technical Lead**: Define security architecture and design patterns; review technical decisions
- **With Developers**: Provide security guidance; conduct code security reviews
- **With Project Manager**: Escalate security risks and compliance issues; coordinate incident response
- **With QA/Testing Lead**: Coordinate security testing and vulnerability scanning
- **With Sponsor/Stakeholder**: Report compliance status and security risks to executive leadership
